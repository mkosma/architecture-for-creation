# 09 Kernel

The compact canonical statement. Everything else derives from it.

## Why this exists

The Kernel is the answer to a single question: if all of this had to be stated once, as tightly as it can be stated, what would it say?

It exists because a body of thought with no canonical center drifts. Each expression is written for its own audience, each drifts a little from the last, and after a few years there is no fact of the matter about what the position actually is. A Kernel makes drift detectable. Any artifact can be held against it and found consistent or not.

It is also generative rather than merely descriptive. The point is not to have a summary on hand for people who will not read the book. The point is that a bio, a talk, a course outline, and a company narrative can each be produced from it, and that a reader who has only the Kernel possesses the Architecture rather than a report about it.

Compactness is the discipline. A short statement forces the question of what is actually load-bearing, and that question is where the real work happens.

## What belongs here

The canonical statement, and very little else:

- the idea in one sentence, then in one paragraph
- the load-bearing claims, each stated so it could be wrong
- the terms of art the statement depends on, defined once
- what it displaces, meaning the prevailing view it is a reaction to
- the boundary where it stops applying
- what is settled, separated from what is still in motion

Version it, so that a downstream artifact can name which Kernel it was built against. Keep provenance, so each claim can be traced to the sessions, cases, or principles that produced it.

## What does not belong here

**The Kernel is not the book.** The book is in `10-narrative/`.

**It is not explanatory.** No worked examples, no build-up, no persuasion, no gentle on-ramp for a reader who does not have the context. Explanation is what expressions are for, and every paragraph of it added here makes the Kernel worse at its actual job.

Nothing that has not been earned elsewhere. A claim appears here after it has survived `04-case-studies/` and been stated properly in `06-distinctions/` or `07-principles/`. The Kernel is the compression, not the place where thinking is done.

Nothing tentative. Open matters belong in `08-questions/`. The Kernel may say "unsettled" about a region, but it does not speculate.

## How it relates

The hinge of the repository. Everything in `00` through `08` feeds it; everything in `10` through `12` derives from it.

The canonical rule applies here in full: **no public artifact may contradict the Kernel.** When an application cannot be written without contradiction, the resolution happens in this folder first, and only then in the application. Amending the Kernel is a deliberate act with a version bump and a note on what changed and why, not an edit made in passing while writing a deck.

Superseded Kernel versions move to `archive/`. The sequence of Kernels is itself a record of how the understanding developed.
