# 01 Biographical Sources

The raw record of a career, kept as evidence.

## Why this exists

A life contains patterns that its owner is the last to see. The same move repeats across a physics lab, a law firm, a consulting engagement, and a startup, and because the domains look nothing alike, the repetition stays invisible from the inside. It becomes visible only when the accounts are laid side by side and read as data.

That is the function of this folder. These documents were written at different times, for different purposes, by a person trying to get a particular job or explain himself to a particular audience. Each one is a snapshot of what seemed worth saying at that moment, which makes the series more informative than any single entry. What persists across twenty years of self-description is likely to be structural. What appears once was probably situational.

Read them as an archaeologist reads strata, not as an author reads a draft.

## What belongs here

Documents that describe the life and career as fact:

- resumes and CVs, every version, including superseded ones
- background descriptions and career summaries
- autobiographical notes
- transcripts, credentials, and records of what was actually done

Keep the original file, whatever its format, and keep it unedited. Where a binary format such as PDF or Word makes the content unreadable to a text search, add a markdown reading copy of the same name beside it, and mark clearly which is the record and which is the convenience.

Preserve the date each document was written. In a series like this, the date is half the information.

## What does not belong here

**These documents are evidence. They are not the Architecture.** Nothing in this folder is a claim about how creation works; it is a record of what happened.

Published biographies belong in `02-public-bios/`, because a bio is a public performance of identity rather than a private record of fact, and the difference matters. Analysis of a project belongs in `04-case-studies/`. Essays and papers belong in `05-essays-and-scholarship/`. And the patterns found by reading these files do not stay here: once named, a pattern is a distinction or a principle and moves to `06` or `07`.

Do not edit these documents to make them consistent with each other or with current thinking. An inconsistency between the 2010 account and the 2026 account is a finding.

## How it relates

This is the empirical base for any claim the Architecture makes about a life. When the Kernel asserts a pattern, this folder is where the instances are, and a pattern that cannot be instantiated here is not yet earned. `04-case-studies/` takes individual projects named in these documents and examines them properly.
