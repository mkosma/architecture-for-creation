# 08 Questions

What remains open, deliberately.

## Why this exists

A good question is more productive than a mediocre answer, and it lasts longer. It keeps a region of the work alive and under observation, whereas an answer closes the region and stops the looking. The failure mode this folder guards against is the reflex to resolve: the discomfort of an open question is real, and it drives people to accept the first plausible closure just to be rid of it.

Holding a question open is a deliberate act, and it needs a place designed for it. Left in a notebook margin or a session transcript, an open question quietly disappears. Given its own file in its own folder, it stays visible, gets revisited, and accumulates the partial findings that eventually make an answer possible.

Many of these should remain open for years. That is the intended behavior, not a backlog to be burned down.

## What belongs here

One file per question, stated as precisely as possible. The precision matters more here than anywhere else in the repository, since a vague question cannot be answered or abandoned and simply persists.

For each, record what provoked it, why the obvious answers fail, what would count as an answer, and what partial findings have accumulated. Where the question has been sitting for a long time, note that too. Longevity is information.

Questions that turn out to be malformed belong here as well, with a note about why. Learning that a question was wrongly asked is a real result.

## What does not belong here

**Do not rush to answer them.** A question closed early leaves the strongest version of itself unexamined, and closing it usually means answering an easier one that was quietly substituted.

Not to-do items. "Draft the R Street bio" is work and belongs in `TODO.md` at the root. A question here is something not yet understood, not something not yet done.

Not rhetorical questions. A question whose answer is already assumed is an assertion, and belongs in `07-principles/` where it can be examined as one.

Not objections to be defeated. When a question exists to be knocked down, it is not open. Record the strongest form of the challenge, including one that might cost a principle.

## How it relates

The mirror of `07-principles/`. That folder holds what is settled; this one holds what is not. A repository with a large `07` and an empty `08` should be suspected of premature closure.

Questions frequently arise from `04-case-studies/`, where a case resists analysis, and from `00-discovery-sessions/`, where they are asked and then swept past. When a question does close, it moves into `06`, `07`, or `09` as whatever it became, and the file here is updated to record where the answer went. Nothing is deleted; the history of an open question is part of how its answer earned its place.
