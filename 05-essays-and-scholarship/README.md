# 05 Essays and Scholarship

Longer writing, gathered so the recurring moves become visible.

## Why this exists

Over a long career a person writes a great deal in service of immediate purposes: a law review article, a white paper, a comment to an agency, a post written in an afternoon. Each was aimed at its own target and then set aside. Read together, and read for method rather than subject, they show something their author did not intend to reveal.

The recurring pattern is what this folder is for. The same structural move appears in a piece about presidential war powers and a piece about scientific reproducibility, though the topics share nothing. The same instinct about where to look first, the same way of locating the constraint, the same preference for the empirical detail over the theoretical frame. Those habits are the Architecture in operation, years before it was named.

This is also the record of what has already been said in public, which matters when the same ground is covered again in a book.

## What belongs here

Substantial pieces of writing, published or not:

- articles and scholarship
- published papers
- blog posts
- white papers
- responses to government RFIs and agency comments
- talks and lectures with a written text

Keep each piece as written, with its date, venue, and audience. Where a piece was revised for publication, keeping both versions is worthwhile, since the edits show what an outside reader would not accept.

## What does not belong here

New writing that expresses the Architecture is not archival material. A new essay aimed at a specific audience is an application and belongs in `11-applications/`; the long-form unfolding of the ideas belongs in `10-narrative/`. This folder is for work that already exists, whatever it was originally for.

Short public self-descriptions belong in `02-public-bios/`. Career records belong in `01-biographical-sources/`. Reading notes on other people's work belong in `03-intellectual-influences/`.

Do not revise old pieces to bring them into line with current thinking. Their datedness is part of what they document.

## How it relates

A second empirical base, parallel to `01-biographical-sources/`. That folder shows what was done; this one shows how the thinking went, which is closer to the Architecture's actual subject.

Feeds `06-distinctions/` and `07-principles/`, since a move that recurs across unrelated pieces over decades is a strong candidate for a principle. Feeds `04-case-studies/` where a piece is the primary artifact of a project. And it constrains `10-narrative/`, which should not claim a realization arrived later than the writing shows it did.
