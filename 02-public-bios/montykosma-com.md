## Stories

I got to ride with Richard Feynman in his famous van, and spent a bunch of hours with the man. He inspired me to take a path through life of diving deeply into everything that sparks my curiosity.

Everyone knew that Justice Scalia always asked his potential law clerks, “what is the worst decision in Supreme Court history?” My co-clerk Steve Seeger, a real joker and a religious freedom scholar, advised me, “just say Employment Divison v Smith” when Scalia popped the inevitable question. (For those non-lawyers out there, Smith was a Scalia majority opinion holding that a facially neutral law prohibiting the use of peyote did not violate the First Amendment even though it was designed to and in fact did single out and prohibit a religious practice.) Silly joke, as there were about 100 better answers to the question. But in my interview, sure enough, Justice Scalia asked the question, and my mind instantly went blank … and I found myself saying, Employment Division v. Smith. It just came out. His jaw dropped. Literally, his mouth hung open. “Now that’s a bold answer, Mr. Kosma! Explain yourself.” I couldn’t believe I said it, didn’t have the presence of mind to laugh it off as a bad joke, or even to blame my cursed co-clerk Seeger. (I felt better when I learned that Peter Thiel also screwed up a Scalia interview, though I bet his story isn’t as funny.) Perhaps one day I should smoke some peyote in memory of late Justice Scalia.

I was there when Michael Jackson’s hair caught on fire. I was parking cars at the Shrine Auditorium for the Pepsi shoot and was hanging around the door when all these little kids came running out, screaming and crying.

More men should do mani-pedis together. My favorite partners have been Miguel Estrada and Elliot Noss.
