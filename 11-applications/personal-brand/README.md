# Personal Brand

How this person is presented, everywhere it is not somebody else's page.

## Why this exists

A career that crosses physics, law, consulting, operations, and startups reads to most people as a series of unrelated jobs. The conventional advice is to pick one and lead with it, which is a way of solving the presentation problem by discarding most of the evidence. The Architecture offers a different solution: the career is coherent, and what unifies it is a way of working rather than a subject matter.

This folder is where that coherence gets stated in the first person. It is the highest-leverage application, because it is the one a stranger encounters first and the one that determines whether anything else gets read.

It is also the most honest test available. Nobody else's editorial constraints apply here. A weak personal statement cannot be blamed on a venue's house style.

## What belongs here

Self-presentation the author controls:

- the LinkedIn profile: headline, About section, role descriptions
- the personal website: copy, structure, and what it is actually for
- speaker bios and conference introductions in draft
- any other first-person account of who this is and what they do

Write for the specific surface. A LinkedIn headline, a website landing page, and a spoken introduction have almost nothing in common as forms, and the same paragraph pasted across all three will underperform in each.

## What does not belong here

Not published versions. The live LinkedIn profile and the current site are captured in `02-public-bios/` as the record of what is deployed.

Not career facts. Resumes and background documents are evidence and live in `01-biographical-sources/`.

Not visual design. The site's typography, layout, and diagrams belong in `12-visual-design/`; this folder holds what it says.

Not a summary of the Architecture. A personal bio that explains the ideas has confused the expression with the source. It should be written by someone who holds the Architecture, and it should not be about the Architecture.

## How it relates

Derived from `09-kernel/`, grounded in `01-biographical-sources/`, and measured against `02-public-bios/`, which shows what is currently deployed and where it diverges.
