# Chironic

The Architecture expressed as a company.

## Why this exists

Chironic AI is the hardest application in the repository, because a company is not only described by the Architecture; it is built out of it. The narrative, the one-pager, and the deck are read by people deciding whether to invest, join, or buy, and those decisions are made on the coherence of the whole thing rather than on any sentence in it. Incoherence is detected long before it is articulated.

That makes this folder a live test rather than an exercise. If the Architecture is sound, it should produce a company story that holds together under the scrutiny of people whose money is at stake. If it produces something that reads well and does not survive a hard question, the problem is upstream and this is where it will show.

## What belongs here

External expressions of Chironic:

- the company narrative, in its long form
- the one-pager, derived from the narrative rather than written in parallel with it
- deck copy and structure
- other material aimed at investors, customers, partners, and recruits

Name the audience for each artifact before writing it. Investors, customers, and recruits want three different documents, and a single narrative pretending to serve all three serves none.

Record which Kernel version each artifact was built against, and keep shipped versions alongside drafts.

## What does not belong here

Not the Chironic case study. The honest internal analysis, including what has not worked, belongs in `04-case-studies/`, where the current investor deck is also held as a primary artifact. Both accounts should be true; only this one is written for the world.

Not operating documents. Product specs, contracts, and financial models are the business of running the company, not expressions of the Architecture.

Not claims that outrun the Kernel. Fundraising exerts steady pressure toward the confident formulation slightly ahead of what is actually established. That pressure is exactly what the canonical rule exists to resist, and yielding to it here would corrupt the source rather than merely oversell the company.

## How it relates

Derived from `09-kernel/`, and in continual tension with `04-case-studies/`, which holds the version of Chironic that does not have to persuade anyone.

The deck at `04-case-studies/chironic-deck-2026-08-15.pdf` predates the Architecture entirely. It is a useful record of what was being claimed before, and it is not a starting point to be edited. New work here derives from the Kernel.
