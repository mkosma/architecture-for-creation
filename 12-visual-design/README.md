# 12 Visual Design

The Architecture given visual form.

## Why this exists

One of the principles holds that beauty is evidence of coherence. A folder of visual work is where that claim gets tested against something that cannot be argued with: a diagram either resolves or it does not, and the eye reports the verdict before the mind constructs a defense.

This is why visual work is not decoration applied at the end. An idea that cannot be drawn is usually an idea that has not been understood, and the attempt to draw it locates the confusion faster than another draft of the prose. Diagramming is a form of thinking here, and it will frequently send work back upstream.

There is also a plainer reason. Many people will meet the Architecture as a website or a diagram and never read a word of the narrative. For them, the visual work is the Architecture.

## What belongs here

Visual expression in any medium:

- the website
- diagrams and schematics
- animations
- designed PDFs
- slide decks
- illustrations

Keep sources and outputs together, and keep the sources editable. A diagram that survives only as a flattened image cannot be revised when the thinking moves.

**Typography matters. Whitespace matters. Beauty matters.** These are not preferences. In a body of work claiming that form and function must align, a document that argues for coherence while looking incoherent refutes itself in front of the reader.

**The visual form should embody the Architecture rather than merely describe it.** A diagram that lists the ideas in boxes is a table of contents. A diagram whose structure is the idea is doing the work.

## What does not belong here

Not text drafts. Copy for the website belongs with its application in `11-applications/personal-brand/`; this folder holds the form. In practice the two are developed together, and keeping the source of each in its own place is what prevents either from being lost.

Not captured artifacts from elsewhere. A PDF of somebody else's page is evidence and belongs in `02-public-bios/` or `04-case-studies/`.

Not visual work that contradicts the Kernel. A diagram makes claims about structure as surely as a sentence does, and a picture implying a relationship the Architecture does not hold is a contradiction, whatever the accompanying text says. The canonical rule applies to images.

## How it relates

Downstream of `09-kernel/` and closely coupled to `11-applications/`, most of which eventually need visual form.

Also, in practice, upstream. Diagramming exposes structural gaps that prose conceals, and when it does, the finding goes back into `06-distinctions/`, `08-questions/`, or the Kernel. Expect this folder to send work backward as often as it receives it.
