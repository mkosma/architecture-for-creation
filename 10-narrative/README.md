# 10 Narrative

The long-form unfolding. The research notebook. Eventually the book.

## Why this exists

The Kernel states the Architecture. It cannot convey how anyone arrives at it, and arrival is most of the value. A reader handed a set of finished conclusions gets information. A reader taken through the discovery gets the capacity to do it themselves, which is the difference between a summary and a transmission.

This folder is where that account is written. It is the story of a set of ideas coming into focus: what was being attempted, what went wrong, what refused to resolve, what suddenly reframed everything, and what it cost to see it. It is written in order, because the order is the argument.

It is also the working notebook. Between the raw transcripts and the compressed Kernel there has to be a place where thinking is done at length, in prose, without the obligation to be either verbatim or final. That middle register is what this folder holds, and the book is what it eventually becomes.

## What belongs here

The long-form account, and the drafts on the way to it:

- the unfolding, in chronological order
- the research notebook: extended thinking, worked-through problems, arguments in progress
- worked examples at whatever length they need
- the side quests, kept in place

Preserve chronology. When a realization arrived late, the narrative should show it arriving late, because rearranging discoveries into a tidy sequence quietly misrepresents how creation works, in a document whose subject is how creation works.

Preserve the side quests. The detour that went nowhere is often what makes the eventual turn intelligible, and cutting it leaves the reader with a conclusion that seems to arrive from nowhere.

Number and date drafts. Keep the earlier ones rather than editing in place; superseded drafts move to `archive/`.

## What does not belong here

Not verbatim transcripts. Those are primary sources and stay in `00-discovery-sessions/`. This folder is authored prose about that material.

Not the canonical statement. The Kernel is short and generative and lives in `09-kernel/`. If the narrative begins functioning as the canonical source, the Kernel has failed and should be fixed rather than worked around.

Not audience-specific expressions. A talk, an essay for a particular publication, a course module: those are applications and live in `11-applications/`, however long they run.

**Optimize for truth rather than polish.** A passage that is honest about a confusion is worth more here than a passage that reads well and smooths it over. Polish is a late operation and a small one; the risk is that it arrives early and quietly removes the parts that were hardest to admit.

## How it relates

Sits between the evidence and the expressions. It draws on everything in `00` through `08`, and it must remain consistent with `09-kernel/`, which it explains but does not supersede.

When writing here surfaces something new, and it will, the new thing does not stay here. It goes into `06-distinctions/`, `07-principles/`, or `08-questions/`, and then into the Kernel if it belongs there. Narrative is where discovery frequently happens; it is not where discovery is stored.
