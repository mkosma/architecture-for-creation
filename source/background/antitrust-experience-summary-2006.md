The following briefly describes some of the matters in which I had substantial  involvement, roughly in reverse chronological order. If you would like the name of someone knowledgeable about my work on any of these matters, please let me know.

J O N E S   D A Y

Procter & Gamble – Acquisitions of Gillette & Wella

P&G’s acquisition of Gillette, the largest consumer products transaction in history, is currently pending at the Federal Trade Commission (FTC). As part of the core team, my responsibilities included, in cooperation with our economists and other lawyers, preparing white papers on competitive issues relating to several different product markets, drafting interrogatory responses, negotiating various issues with FTC staff, and overall planning and management of P&G’s compliance with the FTC’s Second Request.

P&G’s Wella acquisition combined the second and third largest suppliers of hair coloring products to the salon industry. I was again part of the core team helping prepare the factual and legal arguments demonstrating that the transaction would likely create a more effective competitor to the dominant player, L’Oreal, and that the risk of competitive harm was low given the market structure. After we withdrew and refilled the HSR submission to permit more time to address the relevant issues, DOJ decided not to issue a Second Request.

I have also participated in analyzing various product markets and issues and advising P&G in connection with other potential acquisitions.

Cabot – Carbon Black Antitrust Litigation

In late 2002, the European Commission conducted a dawn raid of Cabot’s European offices seeking information relating to supposed price fixing of carbon black. I assisted Joe Sims in conducting an internal investigation at Cabot’s headquarters in the US and advising Cabot regarding the investigation. Shortly thereafter, numerous civil antitrust class actions were filed in both federal and state courts. Working with John Majoras, I helped handle virtually all aspects of the federal litigation, ranging from responses to the various complaints, to consolidation through multi-district litigation proceedings, to coordination of negotiations with plaintiffs, to managing a voluminous document review and production, to depositions, to working with our economists against class certification.

I also took responsibility for a class action lawsuit filed in North Carolina state court. Although an appellate decision in that state permitted indirect purchasers to bring an antitrust lawsuit, we successfully advanced the novel argument that the plaintiff – a purchaser of tires – had an injury that was too remote from the alleged price fixing of carbon black. I prepared the motion to dismiss and participated in oral argument, resulting in dismissal of the complaint. I supervised preparation of defendants’ appellate briefs, and plaintiffs withdrew their appeal on the eve of oral argument.

Dell Computer – Antitrust Counseling

I have assisted Joe Sims in advising our client regarding a variety of antitrust compliance and litigation issues.

Clear Channel Corporation – DOJ Concert Promotion Investigation

Clear Channel received a Civil Investigative Demand from the DOJ seeking information relating to the company’s concert promotion and radio businesses. Along with helping advise our client on various strategic issues, I was responsible for most aspects of compliance with the CID, including researching and analyzing multiple sources of corporate data, designing and executing a plan for collection, review, and production of documents from sources in roughly 25 locations across the United States, and negotiating various issues with DOJ.

Ameren – Acquisitions of CILCORP & Illinois Power, CID

Working with Kathy Fenton, I served as the lead antitrust associate in Ameren’s acquisition of CILCORP, responsible for negotiating various issues with the DOJ, legal and factual analysis of electric power markets and the state of deregulation in Illinois, and preparing and managing Ameren’s response to the Second Request. Ultimately, the investigation closed with no action by DOJ. I also participated in advising Ameren on its acquisition o f Illinois Power and in complying with a CID issued by the DOJ.

Jones Day Electronic Discovery Task Force (2003–present)

As a member of the firm’s E-Discovery task force, I am one of the leaders in evaluating and implementing cutting-edge electronic discovery software and management techniques within the Jones Day antitrust practice. I have published articles and given speeches in this area, and frequently assist with the management of electronic document collection, review, and production, and related negotiations with DOJ and FTC.  For instance, I have recently advised clients and Jones Day lawyers regarding such issues in connection with Nextel’s acquisition of Sprint, Federated’s acquisition of May Company, ChevronTexaco’s acquisition of Unocal, and investigations of Bayer, UPM Kymmene, and Citizens Gas.

ChevronTexaco – Puerto Rico Antitrust / Takings Litigation

The Secretary of Justice of Puerto Rico issued a cease-and-desist order against Texaco Puerto Rico, asserting that its dealer incentive program violated Puerto Rico’s antitrust and gasoline laws. Working with Deborah and later John Majoras, I helped prepare a federal complaint and motion for preliminary injunction alleging that the cease-and-desist order violated the Constitution’s due process clause and constituted a taking of Texaco’s property. We also sought a federal declaratory judgment that Texaco’s discount program did not violate the Commonwealth’s antitrust laws. On the morning of the PI hearing, we achieved a settlement that fully protected Texaco for its past conduct and paved the way for a more productive future relationship with the local antitrust authorities.

Open Travel Alliance – Antitrust Counseling

I advised this industry association in connection with antitrust compliance issues, including application of the Standards Development Organization Advancement Act of 2004.

US Steel – Antitrust Counseling

I participated in advising US Steel in connection with various proposed acquisitions.

General Electric – Antitrust Counseling

I participated in advising GE regarding application of various antitrust doctrines, including multi-product bundling, price squeeze, exclusive dealing, price discrimination, predatory pricing, tying, and unilateral refusal to deal.

Bayer – Antitrust Investigations

I participated in internal investigations, conducted interviews, and analyzed facts in connection with internal investigations and compliance with grand jury subpoenas relating to various rubber products.

RE/MAX – Antitrust Litigation

A RE/MAX competitor adopted a policy that singled out RE/MAX real estate brokers for adverse treatment whenever a RE/MAX broker sold a home that was listed by the competitor. I helped to evaluate the application of the antitrust laws to this situation and to prepare a complaint alleging tying and exclusive dealing.

Foundation for Research on Economics and the Environment – RICO Litigation

My client, the not-for-profit Foundation for Research on Economics and the Environment, conducts educational seminars for federal judges and others. In Prunté v. Disney Corp., (D.D.C., filed April 2004), the plaintiff alleged that he was the author of a song used in Disney’s Hunchback of Notre Dame, and that FREE facilitated a conspiracy among federal judges, the Disney Corporation, and others, to appropriate his intellectual property rights in violation of RICO (and, inexplicably, the Sherman Act). As lead counsel for FREE, I successfully persuaded the court to grant our motion to dismiss for lack of personal jurisdiction.

R.J. Fitzgerald & Co. – Commodities Fraud/False Advertising Litigation

My clients, commodities brokerage firm R.J. Fitzgerald & Co., its owner, and three former employees, were charged with fraud by the CFTC in connection with the firm’s television advertising and promotional seminars. I served as counsel of record in the Supreme Court and lead counsel before the Eleventh Circuit. I was responsible for all strategy, briefing, oral arguments, and mediation efforts in connection with the appeal, rehearing petition, and petition for certiorari. Certiorari was denied, leaving intact the Eleventh Circuit’s unprecedented holding that a person can commit commodities fraud without intending to deceive.

Waterford Multiple Advisor Fund – Litigation Counseling

My client, a private equity fund, was seeking to liquidate, but faced a several million dollar preference claim arising out of a foreign bankruptcy proceeding. After retaining and consulting with foreign bankruptcy counsel and advising Waterford regarding the applicable issues under US law, we enabled its manager successfully to wind up the investment fund.

G I B S O N   D U N N

Northrop Grumman – Acquisition of TRW

The DOJ issued a Second Request in connection with Northrop Grumman’s acquisition of TRW.  I participated in analyzing the transaction, negotiated various aspects of the Second Request with DOJ, and supervised the efforts to interview executives and collect documents in response to the Second Request.

Cadence Design Systems – Mergers & Antitrust Counseling

Cadence acquired Simplex Solutions, Plato Design Systems, and Silicon Perspective, and held substantial discussions with the FTC regarding Synopsys’s acquisition of Avant!. In these matters, I participated in preparation of white papers and other substantive antitrust submissions, coordination and drafting of international merger filings, and various meetings with FTC staff and Commissioners. I also assisted in counseling Cadence on other potential acquisitions, industry standard-setting, and compliance with an FTC consent order.

Teijin – Antitrust Counseling

I participated in counseling Teijin on various antitrust issues, including among other things potential and actual acquisitions, non-HSR-reportable transactions, and structure of sales and distribution contracts.

Ralston Purina Company – Acquisition by Nestlé

As the lead antitrust associate representing Purina in this $11 billion transaction, I participated in all aspects of this matter, including negotiating the scope of the Second Request, working out the assets to be divested in connection with the Consent Order, analyzing substantive antitrust issues, developing related white papers, coordinating interrogatory responses to the Second Request, and drafting letter agreements and stipulations modifying the Second Request. I also coordinated foreign antitrust filings in over twenty jurisdictions, and helped design and manage review and production of over 1,000 boxes of documents from 250 employees. Working directly with Purina’s Controller, I researched and coordinated the production of electronic data from Purina and IRI relating to the relevant products, customers, manufacturing operations, and financials for U.S. and Canadian operations. I was also responsible for coordination among our economic consultants and FTC economists and financial analysts.

Xircom – Acquisition by Intel

I served as the principal associate working for Xircom in connection with its acquisition by Intel. I coordinated rapid responses to various inquiries from FTC staff, resulting in early termination of HSR waiting period.

Daiichi – Vitamins Antitrust Litigation

After the DOJ’s investigation of price fixing by vitamin producers, numerous purchasers filed class actions and opt-out lawsuits in federal and state courts.  I participated in the representation of Daiichi in the civil cases, including working with economic experts, preparing witnesses for deposition, taking depositions, analyzing damages models, managing settlement calculations and payments, and drafting various motions and pleadings.

George W. Bush – Supreme Court Election Litigation

As a member of Bush’s U.S. Supreme Court team in the election litigation, I assisted in editing of the briefs, drafted questions and answers to prepare Ted Olson for oral argument, and took primary responsibility for finding the factual record materials and preparing the joint appendix for the Court. I also coordinated and led the negotiations on record issues among counsel for Bush, Gore, the Florida Secretary of State, and the Florida Attorney General.

Holly Corporation – Longhorn Pipeline Antitrust Litigation

In this case, operators of the Longhorn pipeline sought to recover nearly $1 billion from Holly, which had sponsored a lawsuit by Texas landowners that resulted in an injunction blocking Longhorn from operating its pipeline because of the threat of irreparable environmental harm.  I oversaw a team of lawyers drafting summary judgment briefs based on the Noerr-Pennington doctrine and the First Amendment, helped prepare Bob Cooper for oral argument, and drafted Holly’s briefs on appeal.

Virginia Power – Cavalier Telephone Litigation

In this case, Cavalier Telephone sought to attach fiberoptic cables to Virginia Power’s electric power poles, and filed various claims that Virginia Power was impeding Cavalier from competing and violating its duties under the applicable statutes and regulations. I assisted Thomas Hungar in representing Virginia Power before the FCC and appellate courts, taking primary responsibility for drafting appellate briefs and other filings.

CTIA/CDT – FCC CALEA Litigation

The Cellular Telecommunications Industry Association and the Center for Democracy & Technology challenged FCC regulations on the obligations of telecom companies to support electronic surveillance under the Communications Assistance for Law Enforcement Act (CALEA). Working with Ted Olson, I drafted our briefs and various associated motions, handled communications and relationship issues with our clients, co-counsel, DOJ, and FCC, assisted in moot courts, second-chaired the oral argument, and handled press relations. The resulting D.C. Circuit decision, a substantial victory for our clients, identified procedural and substantive problems with the regulations urged by the FBI and adopted by the FCC.

HP – Inkjet Cartridge Antitrust Litigation

I assisted in preparing appellate briefs on class certification in a California state-court case alleging that HP’s inkjet cartridges incorporated an anticompetitive product design.

Intel – FTC Investigation

As a participant in the team representing Intel in FTC administrative proceedings, I was primarily responsible for coordinating factual investigation and preparation relating to horizontal antitrust issues involving Digital Equipment Corporation.

BMC Software – Neon Antitrust Litigation

After suing its founder and former employees for theft of trade secrets, BMC faced an antitrust counterclaim alleging anticompetitive product design, package pricing, bundling, and predation. I was the lead associate on summary judgment briefing that resulted in dismissal of a significant portion of the case on Noerr-Pennington grounds. Subsequently, I was responsible for coordinating the efforts of three teams of economic experts, assisting in preparation of expert reports, preparing to take and defend expert depositions, preparing BMC employees for individual and corporate representative depositions, and defending depositions. I also designed a document review process and managed a nationwide team of lawyers and economists conducting the review.
