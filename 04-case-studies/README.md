# 04 Case Studies

Real projects, examined through the Architecture.

## Why this exists

A theory of creation that cannot account for actual acts of creation is a theory of nothing. This folder is where the Architecture is put to work on cases whose outcomes are already known, including the ones that did not succeed.

The value runs in both directions. Applied to a project, the Architecture should explain things that were confusing at the time: why a particular constraint kept reasserting itself, why conviction arrived when it did, why a thread that looked peripheral turned out to be load-bearing. When it explains those things, that is evidence. When it cannot, that is a defect in the Architecture, and a more useful finding than another confirming case.

Known outcomes are what make these cases worth the effort. A live project can be narrated to fit any theory. A finished one resists.

## What belongs here

One case per project, each eventually carrying a full analysis in the Architecture's own terms. The projects that warrant treatment include:

Chironic AI, DataJoint, the Tripoli film, The Green Bag, Kindretic, Fandor, and R Street.

Alongside the analysis, keep the primary artifacts the case rests on: the deck as it was actually shown, the memo as it was actually sent, the numbers as they actually were. An analysis whose evidence has been lost cannot be checked later, including by its author.

Treat failures and abandonments as first-class cases. They are frequently more informative than the successes, because the mechanism is more exposed when it breaks.

## What does not belong here

**These are applications of the Architecture, not illustrations of it.** The distinction is strict. An illustration is chosen because it flatters the theory and is written to make a point already decided. An application is an honest attempt to analyze a case, which may fail, and which must be allowed to fail on the page.

A case study written to persuade someone of something is not a case study. Persuasion belongs in `11-applications/`.

Material produced to move a project forward, as opposed to material analyzing it, belongs elsewhere: current Chironic messaging is an application, and the Chironic case study is an examination. Keep them apart even when they concern the same company.

## How it relates

Downstream of `06-distinctions/` and `07-principles/`, which supply the analytical vocabulary, and upstream of them too, since a case that resists analysis is how a missing distinction gets discovered. This is the folder where the Architecture is falsifiable in practice.

Where a case concerns a project that also has an application folder under `11-applications/`, the case study is the honest internal account and the application is the external expression. Both should be true; only one is written to be read by the world.
