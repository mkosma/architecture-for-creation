# 07 Principles

What holds, across cases and across years.

## Why this exists

Distinctions let you see. Principles tell you what is reliably true about inquiry and creation once you can. They are the load-bearing claims of the Architecture, and they are claims: each one asserts something about the world that could turn out to be false, which is what separates a principle from a preference or a slogan.

They are kept separately because they carry a different obligation. A distinction earns its place by being useful. A principle has to be true, and has to keep being true across cases that were not in mind when it was written. That is a higher bar, and it deserves a folder where the bar can be enforced rather than a paragraph in an essay where it can be asserted in passing.

A principle is also what survives translation. Wording changes for every audience; the principle underneath is what every expression has to remain faithful to.

## What belongs here

Enduring principles, stated plainly. The current set includes:

- Reality before theory.
- Observe before explaining.
- Beauty is evidence of coherence.
- Resistance reveals architecture.
- Fear invites inquiry.
- Creation begins with reality rather than ideas.
- Form and function must align.
- The purpose of language is seeing.

For each, record what it rules out as well as what it asserts, since a principle that forbids nothing is decoration. Record the cases that produced it and, when it exists, the case that nearly broke it. Note the conditions under which it might not hold. A principle honestly bounded is stronger than one universally claimed.

## What does not belong here

**Not distinctions.** A distinction is an instrument of perception and belongs in `06-distinctions/`. The two are easy to confuse because a good principle often names a distinction inside it. Test by asking whether the sentence could be wrong. If it could, it is a principle. If instead it hands you a way of seeing, it is a distinction.

Not tactics, methods, or rules of thumb. Useful advice about how to run a meeting is not a claim about the nature of creation.

Not aspirations. "Be curious" describes an intention, not a truth. Principles here describe how things work, not how one wishes to behave.

Not everything asserted confidently in a discovery session. Conviction in the moment is not the standard. A principle has to survive contact with cases that were not present when it was formed.

## How it relates

Distilled from the evidence in `00` through `05` and tested in `04-case-studies/`, which is the only place a principle can be genuinely stressed. Feeds `09-kernel/` directly: the Kernel is largely a compression of this folder and `06-distinctions/` into their smallest coherent statement.

Binding on everything downstream. When an application in `11-applications/` cannot be written without violating a principle here, the conflict is resolved at this level rather than in the application.

A principle that is superseded moves to `archive/` with a note on what displaced it. Principles are not deleted, because the path by which one was abandoned is part of the record.
